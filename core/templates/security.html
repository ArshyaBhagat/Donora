Our Security Commitment

At Donora, protecting your information is not just a priority—it's a fundamental responsibility that guides every aspect of our operations. We understand that organ donation involves the most sensitive personal and medical information, and we are committed to implementing and maintaining the highest standards of data security.

Zero-Tolerance Security Policy

We enforce robust security standards to protect personal and medical data at every step of the organ donation process, from initial registration to post-transplant follow-up.

Our Security Principles:

  • Confidentiality: Ensuring that sensitive information is accessible only to authorized individuals
  • Integrity: Maintaining the accuracy and completeness of data throughout its lifecycle
  • Availability: Ensuring that critical systems and data are accessible when needed
  • Accountability: Maintaining detailed logs and audit trails for all system access
  • Transparency: Providing clear information about our security practices and policies

Our security framework is designed to meet and exceed industry standards for healthcare data protection, ensuring that your trust in our platform is well-founded.

Data Encryption

We employ state-of-the-art encryption technologies to protect your data both in transit and at rest, ensuring that your sensitive information remains secure at all times.

Data in Transit

TLS 1.3 encryption for all data transmission between your device and our servers

Data at Rest

AES-256 encryption for all stored data in our secure databases

Encryption Standards:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS) 1.3: All data exchanged between your browser and our servers
  • AES-256 Encryption: Medical results, user profiles, and all sensitive data storage
  • Encrypted Backups: All backup systems use the same encryption standards

Continuous Encryption Monitoring

We continuously monitor encryption status and for any anomalies or potential vulnerabilities.

Access Control

We implement comprehensive access control measures to ensure that sensitive medical and personal records are accessible only to authorized personnel for legitimate purposes.

Multi-Layered Access Control:

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Users receive access only to data necessary for their specific role
  • Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Required for all administrative and healthcare personnel access
  • Principle of Least Privilege: Users are granted the minimum access necessary to perform their duties
  • Regular Access Reviews: Quarterly reviews of all user access permissions

Authorized Personnel Only

Only authorized healthcare personnel, system administrators, and designated support staff have access to sensitive medical or personal records, and only for legitimate, documented purposes.

Emergency Access Procedures

In medical emergencies, we have secure procedures for authorized medical personnel to access critical patient information while maintaining full audit trails.

Infrastructure Security

Our hosting infrastructure is built with security as the foundation, incorporating multiple layers of protection to prevent data breaches and ensure system integrity.

Regular Audits

Quarterly security audits by independent third-party experts

Vulnerability Assessments

Continuous scanning and assessment of potential vulnerabilities

Secure Configurations

Hardened server configurations following industry best practices

Patch Management

Automated security updates and patch deployment systems

Our hosting infrastructure undergoes regular audits, vulnerability assessments, and secure configurations to prevent data breaches and maintain the highest levels of security.

Incident Management

In the unlikely event of a security incident, we have comprehensive procedures in place to respond quickly, minimize impact, and ensure full transparency with affected users.

Incident Response Process:

  • Detection: Immediate identification
  • Assessment: Rapid evaluation of incident scope and impact
  • Containment: Immediate steps to prevent further damage
  • Investigation: Thorough forensic analysis of the incident
  • Recovery: Restoration of normal operations and data integrity
  • Communication: Transparent reporting to affected users and authorities

Breach Notification Commitment

If a data breach occurs, we will notify affected users and relevant authorities within legally required timeframes. A full investigation and remediation will follow immediately.

Post-Incident Actions:

  • Comprehensive incident analysis and documentation
  • Implementation of additional security measures to prevent recurrence
  • Regular follow-up communications with affected users
  • Independent security audits following any significant incident

User Responsibilities

While we implement comprehensive security measures, users also play a crucial role in maintaining the security of their accounts and personal information.

Account Security Best Practices:

  • Strong Passwords: Use complex, unique passwords for your Donora account
  • Confidential Credentials: Keep login credentials confidential and never share them
  • Device Security: Ensure your devices are up-to-date and protected against malware
  • Regular Updates: Keep your devices and browsers updated with latest security patches
  • Suspicious Activity: Report any suspicious activity immediately

Password Security Guidelines

Never share passwords, one-time codes, or authentication tokens with anyone. Donora staff will never ask for your password or login credentials.

What to Report:

  • Unauthorized access attempts to your account
  • Suspicious emails claiming to be from Donora
  • Unexpected changes to your account information
  • Any security-related concerns or observations

By following these best practices, you help us maintain a secure environment for all users and protect your personal information.

Security Compliance

Donora adheres to national and international security standards and regulations to ensure the highest levels of data protection and privacy.

Compliance Standards:

  • Indian IT Act 2000: Full compliance with Indian data protection laws
  • Healthcare Regulations: Adherence to medical data protection standards
  • ISO 27001: Information security management system standards
  • HIPAA Guidelines: Following international healthcare privacy best practices

Regular Compliance Audits

We undergo regular third-party security audits and compliance assessments to ensure we maintain the highest standards of data protection.

Certification and Validation:

  • Annual security certifications and renewals
  • Regular compliance reviews and updates
  • Staff security training and certification programs
