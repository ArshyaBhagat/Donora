Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Donora, safeguarding your privacy is a foundational commitment that guides every aspect of our operations. This Privacy Policy comprehensively outlines how we collect, use, protect, and disclose the personal and medical information you provide as a donor or recipient.

Free Service Guarantee

All our services are provided entirely free of cost. We never monetize your personal or medical data in any way.

Donora may periodically update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Users will be promptly alerted via their dashboard when substantive changes occur, ensuring complete transparency.

Information Collection

We collect various types of information to provide you with the best possible service while maintaining the highest standards of medical compatibility and safety.

Types of Information We Collect:

  • Personal Identification: Name, contact details, address, and identification documents
  • Demographic Information: Age, gender, location, and relevant lifestyle factors
  • Medical Data: Blood type, current health status, and compatibility factors

Data Collection Methods

All information is collected through secure, structured forms with your explicit consent. We never collect data without your knowledge or permission.

All data is securely stored and transmitted exclusively via encrypted methods, ensuring your information remains protected at all times.

Use of Information

Your information is used solely for legitimate medical and administrative purposes related to organ donation and transplantation services.

Primary Uses:

  • Facilitate accurate medical compatibility matching between donors and recipients
  • Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and legal requirements
  • Provide timely notifications and updates via your personal dashboard
  • Maintain comprehensive medical records for safety and legal compliance
  • Communicate important information about the donation process

Data Protection Promise

We do not sell, rent, lease, or otherwise monetize any personal or medical data. Your information is never shared with third parties for commercial purposes.

Data Security

We implement comprehensive, industry-standard security protocols to protect your sensitive information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Security Measures:

  • Advanced encryption for all data transmission and storage
  • Secure server configurations with regular security updates
  • Restricted database access with multi-factor authentication
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Staff training on data protection and privacy protocols

Our hospital partner complies with all applicable healthcare data privacy regulations, including relevant Indian healthcare laws and international standards where applicable.

Access & Control

You maintain complete control over your personal information and can access, modify, or delete your data at any time.

Your Rights:

  • Access and review all your stored information
  • Update or correct any inaccurate data
  • Request temporary suspension of your account
  • Request complete account deletion and data removal

Data Requests

To request account deletion, data removal, or any other data-related inquiries, please contact us at:

info@donora.ngo

We will respond to all requests within 48 hours and complete most actions within 7 business days.

Legal Compliance

We strictly adhere to all relevant Indian privacy laws, healthcare regulations, and international standards for medical data protection.

Regulatory Compliance:

  • Indian Information Technology Act and Rules
  • Healthcare data protection regulations
  • Medical ethics and patient confidentiality standards
  • Organ transplantation legal requirements

Legal Disclosure

In rare circumstances involving legal inquiries, data may be disclosed only as required by valid law enforcement requests or court orders. We will notify affected users whenever legally permissible.
