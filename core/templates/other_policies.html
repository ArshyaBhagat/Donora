Policy Overview

This section encompasses additional policies and guidelines that complement our core Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. These policies ensure comprehensive governance of our platform while maintaining our commitment to transparency, accessibility, and ethical organ donation practices.

Comprehensive Coverage

These policies cover areas including refunds, transparency standards, accessibility commitments, compliance procedures, and technical compatibility to ensure a complete framework for our services.

All policies outlined here are designed to support our mission of facilitating life-saving organ donations while maintaining the highest standards of service, ethics, and user experience.

Refund Policy

Donora operates as a non-commercial, humanitarian platform dedicated to facilitating organ donation without any financial transactions between users and our organization.

Free Service Model

Since all Donora services are provided completely free of charge, there are no payments to process and therefore no refunds to issue.

What This Means:

  • No registration fees or membership costs
  • No charges for medical coordination services
  • No fees for platform access or usage
  • No hidden costs or surprise charges
  • No payment processing or financial transactions

Our commitment to free services ensures that organ donation remains accessible to all individuals regardless of their economic circumstances, maintaining the altruistic nature of this life-saving process.

Questions About Costs

If you encounter any requests for payment or have questions about our free service model, please contact us immediately at:

info@donora.ngo

Transparency in Donor-Recipient Matching

Transparency is fundamental to maintaining trust and ensuring ethical practices in our organ donation matching process.

Our Matching Criteria:

  • Medical Compatibility: Blood group compatibility
  • Availability: Donor availability and readiness for procedures

Fair and Ethical Process

All matches are based strictly on verified medical criteria and established protocols, ensuring a fair and ethical process for every donor and recipient without bias or discrimination.

What We Don't Consider:

  • Economic status or financial capacity
  • Social status or personal connections
  • Religious or cultural background
  • Political affiliations or beliefs
  • Personal preferences unrelated to medical needs

Our matching algorithm is regularly audited by medical professionals to ensure it maintains the highest standards of fairness and medical appropriateness.

Compliance & Reporting

Donora maintains strict compliance with all applicable laws, regulations, and ethical standards governing organ donation and healthcare services.

Regulatory Compliance:

  • Transplantation of Human Organs Act, 1994 (India)
  • Medical ethics and patient confidentiality standards
  • Data protection and privacy regulations
  • Healthcare service delivery standards
  • Non-profit organization governance requirements

Reporting Violations

If you believe Donora has violated any policies, laws, or ethical standards, we encourage you to report your concerns to us immediately.

How to Report Issues:

  • General Contact: info@donora.ngo
  • Phone: +91 123 456 7890
  • Written Report: 123 Donora Lane, Pune, Maharashtra, India

Our Response Process:

  • Acknowledgment of your report within 24 hours
  • Initial investigation within 48 hours
  • Detailed review and response within 7 business days
  • Implementation of corrective measures if needed
  • Follow-up communication on resolution status

We take all compliance concerns seriously and are committed to maintaining the highest standards of ethical operation in our life-saving mission.
