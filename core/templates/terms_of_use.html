Eligibility Requirements

To ensure the safety and legal compliance of our organ donation services, all users must meet specific eligibility criteria.

For Donors:

  • Must be at least 18 years of age
  • Must be medically fit as determined by our partner hospital
  • Must provide valid government-issued identification
  • Must pass comprehensive medical and psychological evaluations
  • Must not have any disqualifying medical conditions

For Recipients:

  • Must be legally eligible to receive organ transplants
  • Must have medical documentation supporting the need for transplantation
  • Must be under the care of a qualified medical professional
  • Must comply with all medical requirements and follow-up care

Important Notice

Providing false information about your eligibility may result in immediate removal from our platform and could have legal consequences.

User Responsibilities

As a user of Donora, you have important responsibilities that ensure the safety, integrity, and effectiveness of our life-saving services.

Information Accuracy:

  • Provide complete, accurate, and truthful information at all times
  • Update your profile immediately when circumstances change
  • Report any errors or discrepancies in your medical records
  • Maintain current contact information for emergency situations

Medical Compliance:

  • Consent to all required medical procedures through SaveLife Hospital
  • Follow all pre-operative and post-operative instructions
  • Attend all scheduled appointments and medical evaluations
  • Disclose all relevant medical history and current medications

Consequences of Non-Compliance

Misrepresentation, falsification of information, or failure to comply with medical requirements may lead to immediate removal from Donora and potential legal action.

Free Service Agreement

Donora is committed to providing all services completely free of charge to ensure equal access to life-saving organ transplantation regardless of economic status.

Services Provided at No Cost:

  • Platform registration and account management
  • Medical compatibility testing and evaluation
  • Surgery coordination and scheduling
  • Pre-operative and post-operative care coordination
  • Administrative support and case management

This commitment ensures that organ donation remains a purely altruistic act and prevents any commercialization of human organs, in compliance with Indian law and international ethical standards.
