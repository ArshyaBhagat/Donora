Common Questions

Everything you need to know about organ donation and our platform

Is the entire donation process really free?

Yes, the entire donation process on Donora is 100% free for both donors and recipients. From the initial registration to the final operation, there are no hidden fees, consultation charges, or test costs. This includes all the required medical evaluations like blood tests, crossmatching, HLA typing, and any other procedures necessary for safe transplantation.

Our goal is to ensure that finances are never a barrier to saving lives. Thanks to our collaboration with government initiatives and our partner hospital, every medical cost is covered. You don’t need to worry about paying for anything—Donora takes care of it all so you can focus on giving or receiving the gift of life.

Where will the medical tests and surgeries take place?

All tests and surgical procedures take place at our official partner hospital, SaveLife Hospital. This ensures the entire process happens under the supervision of qualified doctors, with professional medical care and legal compliance.

Once a suitable donor-recipient match is found, The schedule for the hospital is then made available on your personal notification section on the Donora website. After you visit the hospital in-person a specific schedule is generated by the hospital, which includes your test dates, evaluation appointments, and operation timeline

How is a match determined between a donor and a recipient?

Matching is primarily based on blood group compatibility

Once a basic match is identified, additional compatibility tests such as crossmatching and tissue typing, etc. are done at SaveLife Hospital. These tests are entirely free, and only after successful results does the hospital proceed to the next steps in the transplant process.
How will I know if I’m selected or matched?
You will be notified as soon as you’re matched with a compatible donor or recipient. Your Donora notification section will show a prominent alert.

After matching, a hospital schedule is generated for you, for in-person registration in the hospital . You can access all this information directly through your notification section at any time.
Who can become a donor on Donora?
Anyone above 18 years of age who is medically fit can register as a donor on Donora. You can select which organ(s) you wish to donate, and your journey begins with a free and simple registration process.

During the registration, a health form will be presented to assess your eligibility. If a match is found, all tests and evaluations required to confirm your suitability are carried out at no cost to you.

What organs can be donated through Donora?

Donora facilitates donation of various organs including kidney, liver, lung, skin. Each type of donation has specific requirements and procedures.

How long does the matching process take?

The matching process begins as soon as you register as a donor or recipient. After which, it can take anywhere from a few days to several weeks to find a suitable match, depending on various factors such as organ type, blood group, and medical compatibility.

Donora uses an algorithm to expedite this process, but it’s important to note that finding a match is not guaranteed. We encourage all users to keep their profiles updated and to be patient as we work to connect donors and recipients.

Is my personal information secure on Donora?

We use advanced encryption techniques and follow strict privacy and security practices to safeguard your personal and medical information. All data is stored on secure servers with multiple layers of protection, and our platform is designed with healthcare-grade security in mind.

We never share your personal information without your explicit consent. Only authorized medical professionals involved in your care have access to relevant medical information. Your privacy and confidentiality are our top priorities.

Donora is dedicated to saving lives through living organ donation awareness, kindness, and collective action. Together, we give the gift of life.
