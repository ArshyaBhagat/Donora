About Organ

An organ is a part of the body that has a special job and is made up of different tissues working together. For example, the heart keeps us alive by pumping blood all over the body, the lungs bring in oxygen and remove carbon dioxide when we breathe, and the brain controls everything we do, like thinking, moving, and feeling. The liver helps clean the blood and digest food, while the kidneys filter waste to make urine. The skin is the largest organ and protects us from germs and injury. Organs work together in systems, like the digestive system or the respiratory system, to keep the body healthy. Some organs can even be donated to help others live, like donating a kidney or part of the liver. Organs are amazing because they keep our bodies working properly every moment.
