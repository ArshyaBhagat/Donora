About Donation

Organ donation is a noble act of donating your organs after death to save others’ lives. It gives patients in need a second chance and spreads hope.

Importance of Donation

Organ donation saves lives every day. By pledging your organs, you give hope to patients waiting for transplants and a second chance at life.

Successful Surgeries

At Donora, every successful surgery marks a new beginning — a second chance at life. Through the dedication of our skilled medical teams and the generosity of organ donors, we have facilitated over 1,250 life-saving transplant surgeries. Each one represents a powerful story of hope, resilience, and humanity. Our mission continues with every heartbeat, every breath, and every life renewed through the gift of donation.

Success Stories

Real stories of hope, generosity, and life-saving gifts.

Ramesh – The Gift of a Second Life

Ramesh, a 42-year-old school teacher, decided to donate one of his kidneys to save the life of his childhood friend, Suresh, who had been battling kidney failure for years. After a successful transplant surgery, Suresh regained his health and returned to his normal life. Ramesh says, “Seeing Suresh healthy again is the biggest reward I could ever ask for.” Their story has inspired many in their village to come forward and register as organ donors.

Pooja – A Second Chance at Confidence

Pooja, a 28-year-old artist, suffered severe burns in a kitchen accident that left her with deep scars and limited movement in her right arm. She underwent multiple surgeries, but her healing remained incomplete. Thanks to a skin donation from an anonymous donor, doctors were able to perform a successful graft that restored not only her skin but also her confidence. Today, Pooja has returned to painting and often shares her journey to encourage others to consider skin donation, calling it “a gift that heals both body and soul.”

Sandeep – Sharing Life in More Ways Than One

Sandeep, a 36-year-old fitness coach, became a living donor when he gave a part of his liver to his cousin, Arjun, who was battling acute liver failure. The surgery was a success, and within months, Arjun was back to living a healthy, active life. But Sandeep’s generosity didn’t stop there. A year later, he also became a skin donor, contributing a small section of healthy skin to help a young burn survivor, Ritu, recover after a workplace accident. Today, Sandeep continues his coaching career while spreading awareness about living organ and tissue donation. “Giving a part of yourself is the most powerful way to change someone’s life,” he says.

Donora is dedicated to saving lives through living organ donation awareness, kindness, and collective action. Together, we give the gift of life.
