About Donora

Connecting lives through compassionate living organ donation. We bridge the gap between willing donors and patients in need, creating hope and saving lives through ethical, medically supervised transplant services.

Our Story
Born from Compassion, Built for Impact
Donora is a technology-driven initiative designed to bridge the gap between willing living donors and patients in need of life-saving transplants. We connect people in need of kidney, liver, skin, and lung donations with verified, ethical donors.

Our platform operates under strict medical supervision and collaborates with top-tier hospital to guarantee that every procedure meets ethical, legal, and healthcare standards. From registration and test scheduling to the final transplant surgery, our process is handled with care and empathy—entirely free of cost.

Through advanced digital tools and a public request board, we ensure complete transparency in donation requests while maintaining strict data privacy. Donora is not just a platform—it’s a movement that empowers individuals to make a meaningful impact.
Our Purpose
Our Mission
Donora’s mission is to eliminate financial, procedural, and logistical hurdles that prevent people from accessing organ transplants. Our goal is simple—save more lives by making donation safe, accessible, and affordable to everyone.

With a strong network of hospital partners, health advisors, and volunteers, we support both donors and recipients every step of the way. No matter your background, you deserve a chance at life—and Donora exists to make that happen.

Our Process
How It Works
The Donora system starts with registration—where donors and recipients create profiles. Users fill out health and personal forms tailored to their needs. Our system matches donors with recipients who have compatible profiles. Upon compatibility a schedule from the hospital is created for necessary registration and testing procedures.

Once a potential match is identified, both individuals are notified and assigned to SaveLife Hospital, our trusted medical partner. Here, necessary tests like blood compatibility, HLA typing, and crossmatching, etc are conducted—completely free of charge. After which the transplantation procedure is scheduled.

Quality Assurance
Medical Supervision
Every test and procedure is performed in our partnered hospitals under expert medical supervision. This ensures that safety, legality, and ethical standards are never compromised.

From blood and tissue typing to surgical evaluation, all services are provided by certified professionals. Donors and recipients are never left guessing—they're guided through every step with full clarity and compassion.
